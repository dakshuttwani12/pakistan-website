Introduction to Pakistan

Pakistan is a country in South Asia. Its official name is the Islamic Republic of Pakistan. Pakistan became independent on 14 August 1947.

Country Information

Capital: Islamabad

Largest City: Karachi

National Language: Urdu

Currency: Pakistani Rupee (PKR)

Independence Day: 14 August

1. History of Pakistan

Pakistan has a rich historical background. The region was home to the ancient Indus Valley Civilization, including Mohenjo-daro and Harappa.

Muhammad Ali Jinnah played a leading role in the Pakistan Movement. Pakistan became independent on 14 August 1947.

2. Geography of Pakistan

Pakistan has mountains, rivers, deserts, plains and a coastline along the Arabian Sea.

  • Karakoram Mountain Range
  • Indus River
  • Thar Desert
  • Arabian Sea Coast

3. Provinces of Pakistan

Pakistan has four provinces.

Punjab

Capital: Lahore

Sindh

Capital: Karachi

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Capital: Peshawar

Balochistan

Capital: Quetta

4. Culture of Pakistan

Pakistani culture includes traditional clothing, food, music, languages and regional festivals. Shalwar kameez is widely worn throughout Pakistan.

Popular foods include biryani, nihari, karahi and chapli kebab. Urdu, Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto and Balochi are among the languages spoken.

5. Economy of Pakistan

Pakistan's economy includes agriculture, manufacturing, trade, transportation and information technology.

  • Agriculture and farming
  • Textile industry
  • Sports goods manufacturing
  • Information technology services

6. Famous Places in Pakistan

  • Faisal Mosque, Islamabad
  • Badshahi Mosque, Lahore
  • Mohenjo-daro, Sindh
  • Hunza Valley, Gilgit-Baltistan
  • Pakistan Monument, Islamabad
  • Quaid-e-Azam Mausoleum, Karachi
